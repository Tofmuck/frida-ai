Why write

Useful reasons to write

The most useful messages are those that concern a specific point on the site, a public document, or the public FridaDev repository.

The following are welcome:

  • Factual corrections concerning the site
  • Questions about a public FridaDev document
  • Remarks on the M6 paper
  • Reports of a problem in the public FridaDev repository
  • References to directly relevant related work

Requests for access to Frida_V4 may be received, but the repository is not currently open publicly.
